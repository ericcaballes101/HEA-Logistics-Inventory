Physical Stock Count & Variance

Enter the physical count for each item counted (leave blank to skip). Each number is saved automatically as you type, so the count is kept even if the app is closed or the page is refreshed. Variance = Physical − System balance at the time of counting. When the count is complete, a supervisor presses Post Variances (passcode required) to record each variance as an adjustment in the stock ledger.

Audit Log — Deleted & Adjusted Items

Every deletion and stock adjustment is recorded here automatically. The log is protected by the passcode and locks again when you leave this tab. Records cannot be edited or cleared from this screen.

Change Delete Passcode

This passcode is required before any item or transaction can be deleted, before stock count adjustments are posted, and to open the Audit Log. It is stored in this browser only.

Inventory Summary Report

Items are grouped by storage location with a subtotal per location and a Grand Total. Opening Balance + Received − Dispatched ± Adjustments = Closing balance for the selected period. Latest Movement is the item's most recent inbound, outbound or adjustment up to the end of the period. Click a location to collapse or expand it. Select a month by choosing a year first.
